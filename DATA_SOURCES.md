# Data Sources and Medical Information Notice

Last reviewed: 2026-05-24

MediGuide China uses static MVP data for navigation assistance only. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any listed hospital. Hospital addresses, phone numbers, services, appointment methods, languages, insurance billing, and waiting times can change without notice.

Users should verify details directly with the hospital, insurer, or qualified medical professionals before relying on the information.

This project does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, triage, treatment recommendations, emergency dispatch, insurance verification, or real-time hospital availability. For possible emergencies, users should contact local emergency services immediately.
